# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality strategy, define testing approaches, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate closely with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to ensure quality gates are met throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approach for projects
- Validate acceptance criteria and collaborate with Product Managers on testability
- Design and execute manual and automated test cases
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Own quality metrics and defect tracking
- Coordinate security testing and compliance validation
- Participate in sprint planning to understand scope and testing requirements
- Work with Developers to design testable solutions and establish test coverage standards

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, high-quality releases
- Reduce defects in production
- Improve test automation and coverage

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Daily standups and testing progress updates
- Quality metrics reviews and risk escalations
- Test case reviews and acceptance criteria validation with Product Managers
- Technical design discussions with Technical Leads and Developers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy and automated test implementation
- **With Product Managers**: Align on acceptance criteria and quality standards
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality status and risks for escalation
- **With Technical Leads**: Discuss testing approaches and quality trade-offs

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business authority, approve resource allocation, and escalate critical decisions or blockers that affect project success. They serve as the voice of business priorities and remove organizational obstacles that impede project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define business context and strategic alignment for projects
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Escalate business-impacting risks and dependencies
- Provide executive air cover and remove organizational blockers
- Review major milestones and release readiness
- Make final approval decisions on scope, timeline, and budget trade-offs
- Communicate project importance and status to upper leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver on business objectives
- Minimize organizational friction and resource contention
- Enable cross-team alignment and support
- Reduce decision-making delays through timely escalations

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates or milestone reviews
- Escalation and decision-gate meetings as needed
- Executive steering committee presentations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provide sponsorship, approval, and escalation support
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and success metrics
- **With Technical Leads**: Understand technical risks and feasibility constraints
- **With all roles**: Remove organizational barriers and provide executive support

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, assess technical risks, and mentor the engineering team on implementation approaches that balance innovation with maintainability. They bridge product requirements and technical implementation, ensuring scalable and maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in design and architectural decisions
- Assess technical feasibility and risks
- Mentor Developers on best practices and code quality standards
- Review technical approaches and recommend alternatives
- Identify technical dependencies and integration points
- Lead technical design reviews and spike investigations
- Collaborate with Product Managers on technical trade-offs
- Advise Project Managers on technical scheduling and resource needs

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate team learning and consistency
- Enable informed technical decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and design reviews
- Technical spike discussions and RFCs (Request for Comments)
- Code reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk and dependency escalations
- Design collaboration with Developers and QA Leads

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical mentoring and architectural guidance
- **With Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With QA Leads**: Collaborate on testable design and quality approaches
- **With Project Managers**: Advise on technical schedules and dependencies
- **With Sponsors**: Communicate technical risks and mitigation strategies

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters (or Agile Coaches) facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach the team on continuous improvement and agile practices. They protect team productivity and foster a culture of psychological safety and iterative delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate and timebox sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Remove team blockers and process impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and collaboration
- Track team velocity and health metrics
- Identify process improvements based on retrospective feedback
- Shield team from external distractions during sprints
- Work with Project Managers to identify scheduling and resource obstacles
- Foster psychological safety and encourage transparent communication

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce process friction and ceremony overhead
- Enable sustainable delivery pace

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Retrospective summaries and improvement tracking
- Velocity and health dashboards
- Coordination with Project Managers on impediment removal

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Collaborate on removing organizational blockers and scheduling
- **With all team members**: Facilitate ceremonies and coach on agile practices
- **With Developers**: Support technical collaboration and reduce silos
- **With Sponsors**: Escalate process blockers that require executive intervention

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Recognize that in smaller teams, individuals may hold multiple roles; in larger initiatives, roles may be distributed across multiple people.
- Reference the cross-role interactions to understand accountability and communication flows.
